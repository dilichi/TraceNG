Our Research & Verification Methodology

How we mapped the Career Routes

We didn't rely on generic internet articles. Our routes are backed by actual market data:

  • Alumni LinkedIn Analysis: We analyzed the career paths of over 1,000 recent graduates across the 5 courses to see where they actually landed 2-5 years post-graduation.
  • Job Board Scraping: We scraped hundreds of entry-level job descriptions from platforms like Jobberman, LinkedIn NG, and MyJobMag to identify the exact skills employers are demanding right now.
  • Industry Cross-Referencing: Routes were validated against the National Universities Commission (NUC) benchmarks and senior professionals in the Nigerian workforce.

Where our Verification Questions came from

Users can claim any skill, but we prove it. Our fact-checking engine is built on proven educational standards:

  • Professional Certification Syllabi: Questions are sourced directly from the learning objectives of global certifications (e.g., AWS, Google Data Analytics, ICAN, PMP).
  • Practical Application Logic: We don't ask rote-memorization questions. We test practical knowledge. (e.g., Asking what df.dropna() does proves a user understands data cleaning, not just theory).
  • Expert Review: The question bank was cross-checked with recent graduates and industry mentors to ensure they reflect actual entry-level interview standards.
