THE PROBLEM

Many students leave school without seeing the road ahead.

Nigerian university students often have limited visibility into the careers their course opens and the steps, skills, and experiences each one needs. Without that, graduates can end up in work that has no link to what they studied.

The TraceNG Goal: We believe that your degree should not be a guessing game. TraceNG exists to turn the invisible into the obvious. By mapping out the exact routes real graduates have taken, we act as your wayfinder—illuminating the hidden paths between the classroom and the boardroom.

We aren't here to give you generic advice. We are here to give you a verified blueprint, so you can stop worrying about what's next, and start building the skills you actually need to get There.

What makes TraceNG different

  • ✓ Real Nigerian journeys, not generic career advice from abroad.
  • ✓ A gap map that compares your self-rated skills with what each route actually demands.
  • ✓ Honest by design: it is a self-assessment, and evidence is shown as “attached”, never “verified”.
